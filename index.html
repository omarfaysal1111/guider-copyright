Personalised Workout Plans

Coaches build and assign structured training programmes tailored to each trainee's goals, fitness level, and schedule.

🥗

Nutrition Tracking

Daily macro and calorie targets, meal logging, and coach-reviewed nutrition guidance all in one streamlined view.

📈

Progress Analytics

Visual charts, body-metric tracking, and milestone history keep both coaches and trainees aligned on real gains.
